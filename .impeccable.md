## Design Context

### Users
Busy sales professionals at Tech Mahindra BFSI APJ (15-person team). Half use it on the run — between client meetings, in transit, logging in an Uber. Half use it at a desk doing post-meeting debrief. One-handed mobile use is the primary constraint. Managers (including the app owner) use it to check team coverage and coach on cadence.

### Brand Personality
Precise · Authoritative · Premium

The app should feel like the digital equivalent of a private banker's toolkit — not flashy, not playful, but unmistakably high-end. A senior executive should glance at a colleague's screen and ask "what app is that?" rather than "oh, another CRM."

### Aesthetic Direction
**Private Banker** — Apple Wallet meets a BFSI command centre.
- Near-black navy hero areas (oklch 12%), warm cream text
- White content surfaces for reading/form-filling
- Client/action cards feel tangible, like physical credit cards
- One strong brand accent (deep navy oklch 36%), used sparingly
- Font: Figtree (variable weight, geometric grotesque, premium, not in banned list)
- Dark navigation bars, white card content
- Urgency expressed through dot indicators and weight, NOT border-left stripes

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over decoration** — empty space is the luxury. Don't fill it.
2. **Typography does the work** — weight contrast (300→800) creates hierarchy without color
3. **Status at a glance** — overdue should be obvious in under 2 seconds without reading
4. **Card as object** — action cards feel physical, dark, tangible. Not colorful gradients.
5. **No border-left accent stripes** — absolute ban. Use dots, weight, background tints instead.
